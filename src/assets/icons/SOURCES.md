# Icon sources

Third-party logos belong to their owners, are shown only to name technologies,
and are not covered by the repo licence
([ADR 0007](../../../docs/adr/0007-split-license.md)). Coloured logos come from
[svgl](https://svgl.app), single-colour marks (`-mono`) from
[Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0), and four from the projects' own
repositories. The Ao mark is an own asset. The inline glyphs in
`src/lib/glyphs.ts` come from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC).
